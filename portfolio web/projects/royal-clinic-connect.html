Case study — 01

Royal Clinic Connect

A bilingual healthcare website for a medical clinic in Lucknow — calm, trustworthy and appointment-first, with services organised for patients rather than marketers.

Category
Healthcare
Platform
Lovable · Vite build
Role
Design & Frontend
Languages
English · हिन्दी

Overview

Royal Clinic Connect presents a medical clinic in Mashak Ganj, Lucknow to local patients. The site opens with a bilingual clinic heading and a clear promise — trusted healthcare with compassionate care — then routes visitors straight to appointment and contact actions.

Services are grouped into scannable blocks: general consultation, health check-ups, common illness care, follow-up consultations, basic health guidance and patient care support. About, “why choose” and contact sections complete the path from first visit to appointment.

Design direction

Trustworthy, clean and professional — soft blue tones and generous spacing signal clinical care without feeling cold, while rounded call-to-action buttons keep the booking path prominent.

English and Hindi headings run side by side so the site speaks to the community it serves, and service cards stay short enough to read on a phone between appointments.
