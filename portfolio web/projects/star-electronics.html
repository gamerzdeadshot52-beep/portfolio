Case study — 04

Star Electronics

A bilingual, product-first website for a local electronics and furniture store — three departments, weekly featured products and store details in a dark, high-contrast interface.

Category
Electronics & Retail
Platform
GitHub Pages · Static
Role
Design & Frontend
Languages
English · हिन्दी

Overview

Star Electronics is a mobile, electronics and furniture store in Ganga, Haryana. The site opens with a bilingual declaration — “Mobiles. Electronics. Furniture. All Brands, One Store.” — and routes shoppers into three departments with call, WhatsApp and store-visit actions close at hand.

Featured weekly products, brand listings, sale moments, the store promise, customer voices and a location block complete the journey from discovery to doorstep.

Design direction

Modern technology aesthetic: a dark, high-contrast canvas with a yellow accent system that reads as energetic retail rather than corporate, and typography large enough to sell from across the room.

Product-forward browsing keeps devices in the spotlight, while an English / हिन्दी switch and a light toggle make the store feel like it belongs to its community.

Technology

Static build Hosted on GitHub Pages Bilingual toggle Dark / light mode
