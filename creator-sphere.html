Case study — 04

CreatorSphere

A creator-management platform that speaks to both sides of every partnership — separate pathways for creators and brands, a six-step growth loop and a fourteen-service catalogue behind a single, confident interface.

Category
Creator Economy
Platform
GitHub Pages · Static
Role
Design & Frontend
Focus
Creators & brands

Overview

CreatorSphere is a creator-management agency site built for two audiences at once. The homepage splits cleanly into pathways for creators and brands — each with its own promise, service list and call to action — so every visitor quickly finds the side of the platform they belong to.

Behind those pathways sits a fourteen-service catalogue across two categories, a six-step growth loop (Discover → Prepare → Match → Connect → Collaborate → Optimize), a four-step process and six working principles, finishing in a contact form that asks up front whether you’re a creator or a brand.

Design direction

A dark, tech-forward canvas with an electric violet accent and large, tight typography that reads as platform rather than brochure. The hero pairs the headline with a live growth-loop diagram — creators, brands and audiences orbiting the CreatorSphere node.

Repetition does the heavy lifting: the creator/brand split reappears in the service categories, the pathway cards and the final contact section, so the structure itself explains the business.
